About the work.

A single right hand rises from a rough integral base, severed at the wrist. The fingers curl in a tense, twisted grip — not a closed fist but a clutch that strains outward, thumb extended. Knuckles, tendons, and the heel of the palm are modelled at nearly half-metre scale, every tool mark visible. Dark bronze patina pools green in the recesses between the fingers.

Rodin isolated and enlarged this hand as an autonomous study drawn from The Gates of Hell. The grand modèle lets the wrist and forearm stump read as monumental fragments in their own right.

This example was cast at the Georges Rudier foundry in Paris in 1962 under licence from the Musée Rodin, signed on the exterior with an interior stamp confirming the museum-sanctioned edition.
