About the work.

A dark-patinated bronze man stands with his right hand on top of his head and his left arm lowered at his side. His head tilts back; eyes are heavy, almost closed. Weight settles on one leg; the chest and shoulders are modelled with anatomical precision at sixty-four centimetres — the second reduction of Rodin's first great nude.

L'Âge d'Airain stunned the Paris Salon of 1877 with its realism; Rodin was briefly accused of casting from life. The model was Auguste Neyt, a young Belgian soldier.

This bronze was cast in October 1945 at the Alexis Rudier foundry in Paris, with the founder's mark at the back and Rodin's signature on the base.
