About the work.

Four muscular men crouch around a central support, shoulders and raised arms bearing a shallow dish above their heads. The material is light beige terracotta, unglazed, with visible fingerprints and tool marks across the bodies and the bowl. Drapery winds between the figures and the central column. One man hunches forward with head down; another reaches a flat palm up into the bowl. The whole piece sits on a tall dark wooden pedestal.

Rodin modelled the figures around 1877 while working for Albert Carrier-Belleuse, who designed the vessel and signed the jardinière. This Arkın example is terracotta at thirty-nine point one centimetres, signed A. Carrier Belleuse — an unglazed cast rather than the polychrome glazed versions in Paris, Cleveland, or Detroit.

Walk around the base and each Titan offers a different angle of strain — bent back, raised arm, tucked head. The bowl is plain; the drama is entirely in the bodies beneath it.
